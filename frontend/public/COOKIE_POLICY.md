Speed Checker ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website (speedchecker.in) to enhance your experience and gather information about how our website is used. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding cookies.

## What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to remember user preferences, facilitate user login, track user behavior, and serve personalized content and advertisements.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### 1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly:

- Authentication cookies - Keep you logged in
- Security cookies - Protect against fraud
- User preference cookies - Remember your settings
- Session cookies - Track your current session

**These cookies cannot be disabled.**

### 2. Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website:

- **Google Analytics** - Tracks page views, user behavior, traffic sources
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited and time spent
- Referral source
- Device information

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking.

### 3. Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to display relevant advertisements:

- **Google AdSense** - Personalized ad serving
- Ad performance tracking
- User interest profiling
- Frequency capping (limiting ad repetition)

You can opt out of personalized ads.

### 4. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services may place their own cookies:

- Social media platforms
- Analytics providers
- Advertising networks
- Content delivery networks

We do not have control over third-party cookies.

### 5. Session Cookies

These cookies are deleted when you close your browser:

- Temporary user preferences
- Shopping cart contents
- Login information
- Performance data

### 6. Persistent Cookies

These cookies remain on your device until they expire or you delete them:

- User preferences and settings
- Language preferences
- Device recognition
- Marketing preferences

## Managing Cookies

### Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through settings:

**Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data  
**Firefox:** Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data  
**Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data  
**Edge:** Settings > Privacy, Search, and Services > Clear browsing data

### Disable Cookies

You can disable cookies by:

1. Opening your browser settings
2. Navigating to cookie/privacy settings
3. Clearing all cookies or specific sites
4. Blocking cookies by default

**Note:** Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

### Opt-Out Options

**Google Analytics:**
Visit: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

**Google Ads Personalization:**
Visit: https://adssettings.google.com/

**DoubleClick/Google Marketing:**
