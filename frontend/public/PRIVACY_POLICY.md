## 6. Cookies and Web Beacons

A "cookie" is a small text file that may be stored on your computer or device. We use cookies to:

- Remember your preferences
- Track website usage patterns
- Understand how you use our Service
- Improve our Service

**Types of Cookies We Use:**

- **Session cookies** - Temporary, deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies** - Remain on your device until deleted
- **Third-party cookies** - Set by external services (Google, advertisers)

**Managing Cookies:**
You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, blocking cookies may affect your experience on our Service.
