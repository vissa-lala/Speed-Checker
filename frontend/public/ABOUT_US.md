## How Speed Checker Works

### 1. Ping Test

We measure the round-trip latency between your device and our server. Lower is better.

### 2. Download Speed Test

Multiple parallel connections download data simultaneously to measure your maximum download capacity.

### 3. Upload Speed Test

Sequential data uploads measure your upload bandwidth capacity.

### Our Testing Methodology

We use advanced techniques similar to professional speed testing services:

- **Parallel connections** - Multiple simultaneous data streams

## Speed Test Basics

### Understanding Your Results

**Download Speed** (Mbps)

- How fast you receive data
- Important for: Streaming, browsing, downloads
- Good speed: 25+ Mbps

**Upload Speed** (Mbps)

- How fast you send data
- Important for: Video calls, streaming, uploads
- Good speed: 5+ Mbps

**Ping/Latency** (ms)

- Time for data to reach server and return
- Important for: Gaming, video calls, responsiveness
- Good latency: <50 ms

### What Affects Your Speed?

✓ **Positive Factors:**

- Fiber/wired connections
- Fewer background applications
- Close proximity to router
- Off-peak hours
- Modern hardware

✗ **Negative Factors:**

- WiFi congestion
- Distance from router
- Multiple simultaneous users
- Background updates
- Network interference
